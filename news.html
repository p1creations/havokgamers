Explore the Fantastical Realm of Nave in Mortal Online 2


×

Date: January 8, 2025

By: p1skates

Category: Game Updates

Mortal Online 2, the persistent sandbox world where players fill the fantastical realm of Nave with content and unique stories, has announced exciting new updates. From January 3rd to January 6th, all accounts that have logged into the game at least once in the past will be able to play for free!

The latest patch, 2.1.3.28, introduces Relic Wars, Guild Heraldry, the long-awaited Broker Search overhaul, Group Tasks, and much more. Players can now compete in ranked duels with a seasonal leaderboard, individual score, rank tiers, and divisions to progress through. Additionally, a new Clade Reroll Token allows players to revisit the character creation screen and change their clade and bloodline, as well as their face and body customization.

Mortal Online 2 continues to evolve, offering players endless opportunities to explore, gather resources, craft goods, set up trade routes, build houses, fortifications, and villages, wage wars, or keep the peace. Forge your destiny in this player-driven fantasy world!

Explore New Weapons in Myth of Empires' Persia DLC



×

Date: January 5, 2025

By: p1skates

Category: Game Updates

The historical survival sandbox game Myth of Empires has revealed a new set of weapons for its upcoming Persia DLC. Indie game developer Angela Game and publisher Imperium Interactive Entertainment have showcased a variety of unique and powerful weapons that will be added to the game.

The Persia DLC introduces over a dozen new weapons, each with distinct designs and abilities. Highlights include the White Wolf Waist Knife, perfect for mounted combat, and the Inferno Hatchet, known for its ability to break through shields and armor. Other notable weapons include the Khorasan Ripple Blade, the Divine Light Spear, and the Sabo Plate Hammer.

In addition to new weapons, the DLC will also feature new shields crafted from Bulat steel and sturdy Suyab vines, providing players with enhanced defense options. The Persia DLC aims to bring even more excitement to the game's already diverse combat system, with guild battles and raids becoming even more thrilling.

With the release of the Persia DLC, Myth of Empires continues to expand its world, offering players new ways to engage in epic battles and build their kingdoms. Stay tuned for more updates as the game prepares for this highly anticipated expansion!




Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III Breaks Records



×

Date: January 3, 2025

By: p1skates

Category: Game News

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III has shattered franchise records, generating over $1 billion in sales within its first ten days of release. This incredible achievement surpasses the previous record held by 2011's Modern Warfare 3, which took 16 days to reach the billion-dollar mark.

Activision Blizzard attributes this success to the game's gripping campaign, intense multiplayer action, and the ongoing popularity of the Call of Duty franchise. Modern Warfare III has captivated players worldwide, with millions engaging in its immersive gameplay and competitive online battles.

This milestone solidifies Call of Duty's position as a dominant force in the gaming industry and sets the stage for continued success in the future. As the franchise evolves and expands, players can expect even more thrilling experiences and record-breaking achievements.
